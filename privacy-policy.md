# Privacy Policy for S-Note

**Last updated:** October 2026

S-Note ("we", "our", or "the app") is a local-first note-taking application designed to respect user privacy.

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
- **Local Storage:** All notebooks, drawings, shapes, and folders created within S-Note are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB.
- **Google Drive Storage:** When you connect your Google Account, S-Note uses the restricted `drive.file` scope. This allows S-Note to create and manage only its own dedicated application folder (`/S-Note/`) on your personal Google Drive. 
- S-Note **does not** access, read, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
- S-Note **does not** operate any intermediate backend servers, user tracking databases, or analytics on your personal notes.

### 2. User Credentials
Your Google account authentication tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any third party other than Google's official OAuth 2.0 endpoints.

### 3. Contact
If you have any questions regarding privacy, contact: `snote.app.dev@gmail.com`
