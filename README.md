## ✨ Features at a Glance

### ✍️ Natural Handwriting & Digital Inking
- **Low-Latency Inking**: Smooth, responsive stroke rendering designed for active styluses, digital pens, and touch screens.
- **Multiple Pen Styles**: Ballpoint, fountain pens, and semi-transparent highlighters with customizable stroke thickness, smoothing, and colors.
- **Palm Rejection & Pressure Dynamics**: Natural variation in line width based on speed and stylus pressure.

### 📐 Smart Shape Recognition
- Automatically transforms rough hand-drawn sketches into crisp vector shapes in real time.
- Supports straight lines, directional arrows, rectangles, ellipses, circles, triangles, stars, curves, and mathematical square roots (√).

### 🪄 Natural Gestures (Scribble to Erase)
- Erase mistakes instantly by scratching over any stroke or text.
- **SHM Gesture Filtering**: Harmonic oscillation detection distinguishes real scratch gestures from fast or cursive handwriting so your continuous words are never accidentally wiped away.

### 🎯 Lasso Selection & Transformations
- Encircle any group of strokes, handwriting, or shapes using the lasso tool.
- Move, scale, duplicate, rotate, recolor, or delete selections with one click.

### 📄 PDF Annotation & Export
- Import multi-page PDF documents (lecture slides, worksheets, contracts).
- Write notes and highlight directly over PDF pages.
- Export your complete notebooks into crisp, vector-rendered PDFs.

### 📚 Notebooks, Folders & Organization
- Organize your workspace with nested folders and customizable notebooks.
- Choose from clean notebook cover designs or set your own custom covers.
- Grid paper options: blank, ruled/lined, dotted, graph, and custom margins.

### 🔒 Privacy-First & 100% Offline
- **Your Data Stays on Your Machine**: No forced accounts, cloud locks, or data harvesting.
- **Complete Backup & Restore**: One-click export and import of all your notebooks and settings.

### ⚡ Seamless Automatic Updates
- Built-in background updates with cryptographic signature verification.
- Manually check for updates anytime under **Settings → Updates**.

## 💻 System Requirements
- **OS**: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- **Input**: Stylus/Pen, Touchscreen, Mouse, or Graphic Tablet (Wacom, Huion, etc.)
- **Storage**: ~50 MB available space
